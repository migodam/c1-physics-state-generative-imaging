## Methodological precedents and what they do NOT establish

**Mixture Density Networks**, Bishop, 1994, NCRG/94/004 (primary report hosted by Aston/Microsoft Research): conditional mixtures solve multivalued inverse mappings without forcing conditional means. This is why an MDN is a serious baseline, not a straw man.

**Recurrent Inference Machines for Solving Inverse Problems**, Putzky and Welling, arXiv:1706.04008: learned recurrent inference is prior art. **Learned Primal-dual Reconstruction**, Adler and Oktem, arXiv:1707.06474 / DOI 10.1109/TMI.2018.2799231: forward/adjoint operators inside learned iterations are prior art. **Deep Equilibrium Architectures for Inverse Problems in Imaging**, Gilton, Ongie and Willett, arXiv:2102.07944: fixed-point learned inverse solvers are prior art. None of their abstract/method scopes establishes calibrated multi-topology Maxwell probabilities.

**Scalable Diffusion Models with Transformers**, Peebles and Xie, arXiv:2212.09748: a Transformer denoiser is not automatically a temporal-memory model. **Analog Bits**, Chen, Zhang and Hinton, arXiv:2208.04202: self-conditioning already provides a low-cost history mechanism. **Flow Matching for Generative Modeling**, Lipman et al., arXiv:2210.02747: learned velocity fields can define a continuous normalizing flow; exact density evaluation still requires its divergence integral and numerical treatment. None removes the need to specify the target conditional material law.

**Automatic Posterior Transformation for Likelihood-Free Inference**, Greenberg, Nonnenmacher and Macke, ICML 2019, PMLR 97:2404-2414: amortized/sequential neural posterior estimation with flexible conditional density models is established. **Sequential Neural Likelihood**, Papamakarios, Sterratt and Murray, AISTATS 2019, PMLR 89:837-848: autoregressive conditional density estimation and simulation allocation are established. **A Method to Model Conditional Distributions with Normalizing Flows**, Xiao et al., arXiv:1911.02052: conditional flow-based inverse generation is established. These methods do not require online evidence integration for every new y, but their training/integration costs have moved offline rather than vanished.

**Particle Flow Bayes' Rule**, Chen, Dai and Song, ICML 2019, PMLR 97:1022-1031: probability transport under observations has prior art. Distinguish processing a genuinely new observation from repeatedly revisiting the same y in a physical reconstruction iteration.

**Diffusion Posterior Sampling for General Noisy Inverse Problems**, Chung et al., arXiv:2209.14687: nonlinear physical residual guidance is an approximate conditional sampling technique. **Practical and Asymptotically Exact Conditional Sampling in Diffusion Models**, Wu et al., arXiv:2306.17775: twisted diffusion SMC uses weighted particles and valid path-space correction; asymptotic exactness is relative to the stated generative target and does not mean a finite gallery has exact mode masses.

**Neural Operator: Learning Maps Between Function Spaces With Applications to PDEs**, Kovachki et al., JMLR 24(89), 2023: operator-learning architectures are established. Discretization-sharing and universal approximation are not guarantees of topology-OOD calibration or high-contrast Maxwell solver stability. A neural operator is a candidate state surrogate/encoder, not inherently a normalized material posterior.

**On the Robustness of Normalizing Flows for Inverse Problems in Imaging**, Hong et al., arXiv:2212.04319: conditional affine-flow inverse instability under OOD inputs is a real architecture concern. Use bounded scales or tested spline alternatives; do not assume invertibility gives OOD reliability.

**On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks**, Guo et al., ICML 2017, PMLR 70:1321-1330: post-hoc probability calibration is established. **Validating Bayesian Inference Algorithms with Simulation-Based Calibration**, Talts et al., arXiv:1804.06788: simulator-consistent checking is established. **Sampling-Based Accuracy Testing of Posterior Estimators for General Inference (TARP)**, Lemos et al., ICML 2023, PMLR 202:19256-19273: stronger sample-based diagnostics are available. Finite tests are not proofs that the real experiment follows the simulator prior; marginal SBC alone can miss conditional errors.

## Novelty map

Already established: probabilistic inverse scattering; many candidates; physical intermediate states; learned iterations; Transformers in diffusion; recurrent memory; explicit mixture weights; sequential weighted particles; posterior distillation; physical residual guidance; 3-D generative EM; current-consistent generative EM (at least at abstract level for EDMA).

Potential C1 contribution: a material-first, branch-contrast-aware Maxwell state mechanism with a well-defined probability output and a matched-total-cost advantage; a precise frozen-family contrast-sufficiency theorem with explicit nonlinear/covariance limits; support-aware mode management whose implementation preserves probability accounting; and trustworthy finite-scale topology reference tests extending to genuine vector Maxwell.

Not yet established: any C1 advantage, any 3-D C1 reconstruction, any trained C1 probability calibration, any inexpensive global nonlinear posterior certificate, or any claim that historical attention is necessary.
